# Human responsibilities — Huolitsija, Finland

Reviewed against primary sources on 24 October 2026. Scope: a Finnish private limited company arranging business freight, with Autopilot performing routine operations. **Zero employees is compatible with this operating model; zero accountable people is not.** An owner/director or contracted qualified provider can fulfil many responsibilities. The company, its board, customers and authorities retain their legal roles.

This matrix distinguishes a requirement for a natural person, a company obligation that can be delegated/automated, and a prudent product control. An app label `legal_point` does not itself prove that legislation mandates a human click for every shipment. No legal opinion, licence grant or incorporation has been obtained by this task.

| Point / kohta | What is legally required | Human action and automation boundary | Evidence to retain |
|---|---|---|---|
| Board / hallitus | Directors must be eligible natural persons. A small board needs a deputy under the statutory composition rule. The board remains responsible for administration and oversight. | Appoint actual directors/deputy; document delegation and supervision. Software cannot hold a board seat. No employment contract is inherently required for a board member. | Register extract, appointments, board minutes and powers. [Companies Act, ch. 6 §§8–10](https://finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2006/624); [PRH responsibilities](https://prh.fi/en/presentation_and_duties/uutislistaus/news/2026/llc-responsibilities-31.8.2026.html). |
| Incorporation / perustaminen | Founders execute the memorandum; required statements and registration must be signed by the designated people. | Founders and directors use their own authorised identity. Automation may prepare documents, but must not impersonate signers. A company in formation is not yet the registered contracting entity. | Memorandum, signed declarations, accepted registration. [PRH guided incorporation](https://www.prh.fi/en/companiesandorganisations/yrityksen_perustaminen/osakeyhtio/guided-package-online.html). |
| Representation and signatures / edustaminen | Board representation and any granted representation rights must be valid. | Owner/director grants scoped authority. Routine electronic acceptances may be generated within an authorised process; there is no general rule that every freight contract needs a fresh handwritten signature. Acts requiring a specified personal signature stay with that signer. | Mandate, scope, expiry, identity and audit trail. [PRH representation rights](https://www.prh.fi/en/companiesandorganisations/yrityksen_perustaminen/osakeyhtio/representation-rights.html). |
| VAT registration / ALV-rekisteröinti | Register where VAT law requires; assess voluntary registration and special transactions. | Authorised representative registers and maintains tax authority mandates. Return preparation/transmission can be delegated or automated through authorised interfaces. This is an accountability/onboarding point, not a legal requirement to manually approve each invoice. | VAT decision, effective date, tax mandates, filed-return receipts. [Vero VAT registration](https://www.vero.fi/en/businesses-and-corporations/taxes-and-charges/vat/how-to-register-for-vat/); [Vero authorisations](https://www.vero.fi/en/About-us/contact-us/efil/authorisations/). |
| Annual accounts / tilinpäätös | Board and managing director, if any, sign financial statements; company governance approves/adopts them as applicable. | An accountant may prepare books and software may reconcile entries. Statutory signers still sign; an auditor is required only when applicable thresholds or other rules require one. | Signed statements, meeting decision, filing receipt. [Accounting Act, ch. 3 §7](https://finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/1997/1336); Companies Act ch. 5. |
| Customs mandate / tulliedustus | Representative must be empowered and state direct/indirect representation; declaration responsibility remains with relevant parties. | Customer’s authorised representative grants the customer mandate. Huolitsija’s authorised officer grants internal standing delegation separately. Routine filing can then be automated within both scopes. An internal toggle is not the customer’s power of attorney or a Customs authorisation. | Customer POA, representation type, EORI, internal delegation, filing identity and receipts. [Union Customs Code arts. 15, 18–19](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/ENG/ALL/?uri=celex%3A32013R0952). |
| Indirect representation / välillinen edustus | Declarant and represented party may be jointly liable for customs debt. | Confirm mandate, exposure and guarantee with an authorised officer. Product requires owner review when standing scope excludes indirect representation; the law does not itself require a new human approval on every correctly authorised declaration. | Agreement, guarantee and applicable delegation. [UCC arts. 77(3), 84](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/ENG/ALL/?uri=celex%3A32013R0952). |
| AML applicability and accountable officer / rahanpesulaki | Duties apply to the businesses enumerated in the AML Act. Freight forwarding alone does not establish obliged-entity status. Insurance distribution, regulated financial services or other activities can change the assessment. | Record an activity-specific applicability assessment. If in scope, designate accountable management/compliance arrangements under ch. 9 §1 and approve risk procedures. Do not call routine KYB approval a universal statutory human duty. | Assessment naming actual services and supervisor; board-approved policy. [AML Act 444/2017, ch. 1 §2, ch. 2 §3, ch. 9 §1](https://data.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2017/444). |
| PEP / poliittisesti vaikutusvaltainen henkilö | For an obliged entity, senior-management approval is required for starting/continuing the relevant PEP relationship; source-of-wealth/funds and monitoring requirements apply. | Qualified senior management decides. App’s `pep_relationship` hold is retained as policy even while AML applicability is unresolved. A PEP match alone is not proof of unlawful conduct. | Identity match, grounds, management decision, ongoing monitoring. [AML Act ch. 3 §13](https://data.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2017/444). |
| Suspicion report / epäilyttävä liiketoimi | If obliged, report suspicious transactions promptly and observe non-disclosure. | Accountable authorised reporter handles FIU/goAML and confidentiality. The legal obligation is reporting by the entity, not a general ban on technical automation; this app deliberately does not file reports automatically. | Restricted report/receipt and decision; never customer-visible tipping-off. Correct basis: **AML Act ch. 4 §§1,4**, not the earlier draft’s ch. 9 reporting citation. [Act](https://data.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2017/444). |
| Sanctions / pakotteet | Prohibited dealings cannot proceed; applicable freezes and reporting must be honoured. | A competent person resolves uncertain identity matches and licensing exceptions under app policy. Neither model confidence nor owner approval can legalise a prohibited transaction. Human review of every clean match is not a general statutory rule. | Current list/version, identity evidence, decision and any authority permission. [EU sanctions regulation 269/2014 art. 2](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2014/269/oj); [Finnish MFA sanctions](https://um.fi/international-sanctions). |
| Dual-use / kaksikäyttö | Authorisations and catch-all/end-use duties apply according to goods, destination, end-user and role. Not every dual-use shipment needs an individual licence: general/global licences and conditions must be assessed. | Export-control specialist establishes classification, licence coverage and restrictions. Software may prepare/check records; only the authority grants a licence. | Technical classification, licence and conditions, end-use evidence. [MFA guidance](https://um.fi/vientivalvontaan-liittyvat-palvelut-ja-ohjeet); [EU 2021/821](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2021/821/oj). |
| Dangerous goods / vaaralliset aineet | Applicable duties require trained/qualified people and, for covered road/rail activities, a safety adviser, subject to exemptions. | Engage a qualified adviser/service provider if needed and assign trained persons for the actual modal functions. Product’s per-booking DG gate is a control, not a claim that the exact same personal signature is universally required across ADR, IMDG and air transport. | Qualifications, appointment/exemption analysis, shipper declaration, acceptance record. [Traficom safety adviser](https://www.traficom.fi/fi/liikennejarjestelma/vaarallisten-aineiden-kuljetus/vaarallisten-aineiden-kuljetusten-turvallisuusneuvonantaja). |
| Insurance and claims / vakuutus ja vahingot | Distribution authority and actual insurer terms determine permitted sales, binding and settlement. | Authorised insurance contact establishes distribution arrangement, insurer authority and delegated settlement limit. `claim_above_insurance_limit` is a contractual/policy gate; no universal statutory euro threshold exists. Redkik sandbox certificates confer no cover. | Insurance/distribution contract, policy, bind receipt, insurer settlement consent. [Insurance Distribution Act 234/2018](https://finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2018/234); [Insurance Contracts Act 543/1994](https://finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/1994/543). |
| GDPR human intervention / tietosuoja | GDPR art. 22 applies to solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on natural persons, subject to specified exceptions and safeguards. | Provide meaningful human intervention where required; assess sole traders/personal guarantees. Name an accountable controller contact. A DPO is required only if art. 37 criteria apply, and may be contracted. Do not assume every company credit decision falls outside GDPR. | Assessment, review channel, authorised reviewer, request/response record, DPIA if required. [GDPR arts. 22, 35, 37–39](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/). |

## Finnish operating rule

Autopilotti hoitaa tavanomaiset toimeksiannot annettujen valtuuksien rajoissa. Omistaja/hallitus vastaa yhtiön hallinnosta ja valtuutuksista. Pätevyyttä edellyttävät tehtävät voidaan antaa sopimuspalveluntarjoajalle. Pakotetta, puuttuvaa lupaa tai puuttuvaa asiakasvaltakirjaa ei voi ohittaa omistajan hyväksyntäpainikkeella. PEP- ja rahanpesuvelvoitteiden soveltuminen arvioidaan todellisen toiminnan perusteella. Työntekijättömyys ei poista näitä vastuita.

## Mapping to the application

`backend/core/legal_points.py` and `/api/autopilot/legal-points` enumerate app holds. `customs_filing_not_delegated`, `customs_indirect_representation`, `sanctions_potential_match`, `ru_by_trade`, `dual_use_export`, `pep_relationship`, `claim_above_insurance_limit`, `director_signature`, `dangerous_goods`, `aml_suspicion_report` are control identifiers, not ten independently proven legal mandates for a human click. The runtime catalogue still contains older legal wording; this document corrects its classification without weakening enforcement. Updating that catalogue is coordinated with s2, not silently changing decision gates here.

Before commercial launch: assign the named legal representative, tax/customs delegate, privacy reviewer, applicable DG specialist and insurance contact; preserve their authority and availability. No person has been assigned by this document. Verify the law and authority requirements effective on the actual filing date; do not rely on an unverified claim that a newer regulation repealed the cited UCC articles.
